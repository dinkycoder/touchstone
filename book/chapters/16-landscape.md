# 16. The agent-payments landscape

Every figure in this chapter carries an "as of" date and a source from the
[evidence log](../research/evidence-log.md).

**By the end, the reader can:**
- size today's agent payments honestly, and say why they're small
- sort the companies working on agent trust by what they actually check
- explain why Touchstone complements Coinbase's facilitator instead of competing with it

## Outline

1. **How much of it is real.** Total x402 volume versus the share that's plausibly agent-driven, and why
   those numbers differ so much.
2. **Who checks what.** Sorted by what each checks rather than by company:
   - identity tokens for agents
   - verification of the business behind an agent
   - address-level screening
   - wallet reputation scores
   - entity resolution (Touchstone's category)

   Candidates to cover include t54 Labs, Skyfire, Baselayer, Catena Labs, the card networks' agent
   programs, TRM Labs and Chainalysis, and wallet-reputation scorers. Only name them after H8 is checked.
3. **The platform in the middle.** Coinbase's facilitator and its built-in address screening, and why
   Touchstone sells to it rather than against it.
4. **What would change the map.** Coinbase adding entity resolution; card-network agent programs moving
   onchain; agent volume staying flat.
