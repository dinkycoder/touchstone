# Preface

Written last, once the book knows what it is.

## Outline

1. **Why this book.** Software agents have started paying each other in stablecoins, one API call at a
   time. Nobody can yet say who is behind most of those wallets, and sanctions law doesn't care whether
   the counterparty is a person or a program.
2. **Who it's for.** Engineers new to blockchains, compliance people new to agents, and anyone who wants
   to see a real system built from first principles, mistakes included.
3. **How it was written.** Built and written together: the founder as author, domain expert and learner;
   Claude as lead writer, researcher, fact-checker and quizmaster. Say this plainly (the wording is an
   open decision in [book/README.md](../README.md)).
4. **Not legal advice.** The book explains how sanctions screening works and how one system implements
   it. It doesn't tell anyone what the law requires of them.
5. **How to read it.** Part I is the problem. Part II is the foundations; skip what you know. Part III is
   the build, sprint by sprint. Part IV is the market, and it's dated on purpose.
6. **Acknowledgements.**
